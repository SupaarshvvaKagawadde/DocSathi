## Meaning

The outer folder represents one application packet.
The interior rails represent separate pieces of supporting evidence.
The final rail turns into a teal tick to represent a checked connection between a requirement and evidence.
The open gap represents review still being possible; the mark must not become a promise of institutional acceptance.
The concept is intended to feel calm, precise and usable in a document-focused product.

## Proposed palette and production rules

Proposed navy: #08254B.
Proposed teal: #00B7A5.
Proposed light canvas: #F7FAFC.
These are design targets; generated raster pixels may differ.
Test contrast for each actual UI use rather than treating brand colors as universally accessible.
Use navy for primary type and teal for limited accents.
Do not rely on teal alone to express a passed check.
Maintain clearspace of at least half the symbol height around the standalone mark.
Plan a 24-pixel minimum icon test and a wordmark readability test on actual mobile devices.
The raster concept has soft tonal variation; production vector artwork should remove unintended shading and refine geometry.
Commission a clean vector redraw with stable strokes, controlled spacing and correct wordmark licensing.
Plan light, dark, monochrome, icon-only, horizontal and stacked variants during production.
Do not assume the generated raster is already a complete brand identity system.

## Clearance before startup brand spending

The concept was created independently from a textual brief without a reference logo.
Original generation does not establish trademark availability or worldwide uniqueness.
Search exact and similar names, phonetic variants and visually similar marks in relevant goods and services.
Use WIPO and the official national registers for planned launch markets, with professional review where appropriate.
Check domain and social-name availability independently; no domain was purchased or claimed here.
If a conflict appears, revise the concept before investing in public campaigns or registration.
